# Owl Store Privacy Policy

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Note: This is an example privacy policy used for representation purposes only during a Twilio Forge event and does not represent a real company.

**Effective Date:** May 4, 2026  
**Last Updated:** May 4, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Owl Store ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you shop on our website, mobile application, or in-store kiosks (collectively, the "Services").

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information
We collect personal information that you provide directly to us, including:
- Name, email address, and phone number
- Shipping and billing addresses
- Payment information (credit/debit card details, digital wallet identifiers)
- Account username and password
- Size preferences and wishlists

### 2.2 Purchase and Browsing Data
To personalize your shopping experience, we collect:
- Order history and purchase records
- Items viewed, added to cart, or saved for later
- Product ratings, reviews, and feedback
- Search queries and filter selections

### 2.3 Device and Usage Information
We automatically collect:
- Device information (model, operating system, unique identifiers)
- Browser type and version
- IP address and general geographic location
- App usage data, session duration, and interaction patterns
- Referral URLs and exit pages

### 2.4 Communications
We may collect:
- Customer support tickets and chat transcripts
- Responses to surveys and promotional contests
- Social media interactions with our brand
- SMS and email opt-in preferences

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1 Fulfill Orders and Provide Services
- Process and ship your orders
- Send order confirmations, shipping notifications, and delivery updates
- Manage returns, exchanges, and refunds
- Provide customer support

### 3.2 Personalize Your Experience
- Recommend products based on your browsing and purchase history
- Display relevant promotions and offers
- Remember your size preferences and saved items
- Customize your in-app and on-site experience

### 3.3 Marketing and Communications
- Send promotional emails, SMS messages, and push notifications (with your consent)
- Invite you to loyalty programs, early access events, and sales
- Conduct surveys and collect product feedback

### 3.4 Analytics and Improvement
- Analyze shopping trends and site performance
- Identify and resolve technical issues
- Develop new product categories and features
- Improve inventory forecasting and logistics

### 3.5 Legal and Safety
- Comply with applicable laws and regulations
- Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
- Enforce our Terms of Service
- Respond to legal requests and protect our rights

## 4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

### 4.1 Service Providers
We share information with trusted third-party service providers who help us:
- Process payments and prevent fraud
- Fulfill and ship orders
- Provide customer support tools and live chat
- Deliver email, SMS, and push notification campaigns
- Perform analytics and A/B testing

### 4.2 Business Partners
With your consent, we may share information with:
- Co-branded loyalty and rewards program partners
- Third-party brands whose products we carry
- Advertising networks for targeted marketing

### 4.3 Legal Requirements
We may disclose information when required by:
- Legal process or valid government requests
- Court orders or subpoenas
- Law enforcement investigations
- Protection of rights, property, or safety of Owl Store or others

### 4.4 Business Transactions
In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to:
- Maintain your account and order history
- Comply with legal and tax obligations
- Resolve disputes and enforce agreements
- Improve our Services

Order records are retained for a minimum of 7 years for tax and compliance purposes. Account data is retained until you request deletion or your account has been inactive for 5 years.

## 6. Your Privacy Rights

### 6.1 Access and Control
You have the right to:
- Access and review your personal information via your account dashboard
- Update or correct inaccurate data
- Download your information in a portable format
- Request deletion of your account and associated personal data

### 6.2 Marketing Opt-Out
You can opt out of marketing communications by:
- Clicking the unsubscribe link in any promotional email
- Replying STOP to any marketing SMS
- Adjusting notification preferences in the Owl Store app or website settings
- Contacting our support team

### 6.3 Cookies and Tracking
You can manage cookie preferences through:
- Your browser settings
- Our on-site cookie preference center
- Opt-out tools provided by our advertising partners

## 7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:
- TLS encryption for all data in transit
- Encryption of sensitive data at rest
- PCI DSS-compliant payment processing
- Multi-factor authentication for staff and admin systems
- Regular security audits and penetration testing
- Strict access controls and employee training

Despite these measures, no system is completely secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your account.

## 8. International Data Transfers

Owl Store operates globally. Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. We rely on standard contractual clauses and other approved mechanisms to ensure appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us.

## 10. Third-Party Links and Services

Our Services may contain links to third-party websites or integrate with social media platforms. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those third parties. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies before sharing your information.

## 11. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including:
- Right to know what personal information is collected, used, shared, or sold
- Right to delete personal information we hold about you
- Right to opt out of the sale of your personal information
- Right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

To submit a CCPA request, contact us at the information below or use the "Your Privacy Choices" link in our website footer.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through:
- Email notifications to registered account holders
- Prominent notice on our website and app
- Updated effective date at the top of this document

Continued use of our Services after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
